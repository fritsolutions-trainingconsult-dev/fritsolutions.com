Quick answers about our courses, registration, and services.

What courses do you offer?

We offer eight practical courses: Microsoft Office Suite, Graphic Design, Intro to Programming, Data Analytics, Basic Computing, Software Development, Web Development, and Artificial Intelligence.

How do I register for a course?

Choose a course in the Courses section, or pick one in the registration form, enter your details, and send your registration. Your email app opens with everything filled in.

Yes. We design customized training and workshops for companies, institutions, and public sector organizations, along with ICT consultancy and capacity-building programs.
